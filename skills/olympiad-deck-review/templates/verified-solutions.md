# Grade 7 Olympiad: Motion and Light
### All 8 questions, independently solved and verified

**File:** `G7-Motion-and-Light.pptx` · 24 slides · 8 questions
**Method:** every question solved from scratch before comparing with the key. Figures read from

| # | Slides | Section | Topic | Key |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 7-9 | Light | Reflection from a plane mirror | C |
| 5 | 14-15 | Motion | Average speed over two legs | B (needs fix, see below) |

## Light
### Q3 (slides 7-9): reflection from a plane mirror · **(C) 50°**
- The angle is measured from the **normal**, not from the mirror surface. The ray makes 40° with
  the surface, so it makes 90° - 40° = 50° with the normal. Angle of reflection = angle of incidence = 50°.
- **Say this:** "Angles in reflection always start from the normal. If you are given the angle with
  the mirror, subtract from 90 first."
- (A) 40° is the angle with the surface: the trap. (B) 80° adds the two angles, forgetting the law. (D) 130° uses 180 - 50.
- Student may ask: "Why the normal?" Answer: it is the one line that does not depend on how the mirror is tilted.

## Motion
### Q5 (slides 14-15): average speed over two legs · **20 km/h**
- Average speed = total distance ÷ total time = 20 km ÷ 1 h = 20 km/h.
- ⚠️ As printed, no option shows 20 km/h. The key (B, 10 km/h) comes from dividing by 2. Concession
  script: "You are right, the printed answer is wrong. The correct method gives 20 km/h; the deck is being fixed."
