# Fixes Log Template (for the deck's creators)

Written AFTER the fixes are applied. It lets the creators check every change without opening
the deck blind. Copy the block below into `<Deck> - Fixes Log.md` and fill it in.

Rules: only content fixes; every fix is listed; image fixes get an honest "best effort" note;
say what was deliberately left alone.

# Fixes Log - Grade 7 Practice Test: Motion and Light

**Deck:** `G7-Motion-and-Light (reviewed v1 - content fixes).pptx`, built from a fresh download of the original · 24 slides · 8 questions

**Summary:** 7 of 8 keys were correct. Q5 had no correct option and is fixed. One working step on
slide 9 was wrong and is fixed. A ray diagram was corrected (best effort, see below). Nothing was renumbered.

> The original deck is unchanged. All edits are in the .pptx above. The original animations are

## 1. Q5 (slides 14-15): no option gave the average speed
**Problem:** the stem gives 12 km + 8 km in 1 hour in total (two legs of 30 minutes), so the average speed is 20 km/h. The old key (B, 10 km/h)
came from dividing by 2 instead of 1.
**Fix:** option B now reads **"20 km/h"**. Slide 15's working now reads "20 km ÷ 1 h = 20 km/h".
**Why this fix:** one option and one line changed. Options A, C and D still test real slips and are
unchanged. We rejected changing the stem because it would make Q5 a copy of the example on slide 6.

  arrowhead points away. Checked at 105 dpi on the rendered slide. Not checked: how it looks in

## Left alone on purpose
